- [roadmap-review.md](roadmap-review.md): the gap review that produced roadmap items 12 to 23.
- [kernel-plan.md](kernel-plan.md), [kernel-plan-2.md](kernel-plan-2.md), [kernel-plan-3.md](kernel-plan-3.md): kernel plans, most of which shipped (see [../kernels.md](../kernels.md)).
- [deltanet-prefill-research.md](deltanet-prefill-research.md), [deltanet-prefill-spec.md](deltanet-prefill-spec.md): the research and spec for the register-resident DeltaNet kernels.
- [research/](research/): early research notes on decode overhead and the wire, exact forward-pass ideas, a Mac and Metal plan, the network scheduler, and the prefill GEMM v2 design.
- [handoff/](handoff/): handoff notes between work sessions.
